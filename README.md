SmartCabin
Building AI course project
Summary
Help people find cabins that offer the best value for their budget. The system estimates a cabin’s price based on its size, sauna, distance to water, number of indoor bathrooms, and distance to nearby neighbors.
Background
- Buying a cabin is a major expense, and comparing prices can be difficult.
- Cabins with similar prices may differ in important features.
- Buyers need a simple way to identify cabins that may be overpriced or good value.
How is it used?
The user enters the cabin’s details and budget. The AI estimates the cabin’s price and compares it with the asking price. It can then flag cabins that appear to offer good value or seem overpriced. The estimate supports the buyer’s decision but does not replace an inspection or professional valuation.
Data sources and AI methods
A dataset of cabins is needed, including their features and recent sale prices. Linear regression can be used to estimate prices from the cabin features. The system can compare the estimated price with the asking price to highlight possible bargains.
Challenges
The estimate depends on having enough accurate and up-to-date data. Features such as the cabin’s condition, view, renovations, and local market demand may affect its price but may not be included in the model.
What next?
- Add more cabin features, such as condition and renovation history.
- Include data from more locations and recent sales.
- Test how accurately the model predicts prices for cabins it has not seen before.
